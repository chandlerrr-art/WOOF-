# WOOF

WOOF is a community-powered rescue and emergency coordination app for injured, stranded, or vulnerable stray dogs and cats. It helps people quickly report an animal in distress, find nearby care options, and connect with local rescuers, veterinarians, and community responders.

WOOF is designed for real-world rescue situations: someone sees an animal in need, creates a report with context and location, and the wider local network can respond with updates, assistance, or transport to a care center.

## What WOOF does

- Lets people report an animal in need with details such as urgency, description, location, and photo.
- Surfaces a live public board of rescue reports so local responders can discover cases nearby.
- Supports a responder workflow where volunteers can sign in, claim reports, and update case status.
- Helps users find nearby veterinary clinics, animal shelters, and emergency care options.
- Provides first-aid and safety guidance for common rescue situations.
- Gives a simple way to track an animal's journey from "reported" to "connected" to "help arrived" or "resolved."

## Core features

### Rescue reporting
WOOF allows a citizen or rescuer to report an animal in need with key details: type of case, location, urgency, and notes. Reports can be shared across the community so nearby responders can see what is happening.

### Local emergency hub
The app includes a local care directory for finding nearby animal-help resources and facilities. This makes it easier to connect an animal with the right form of care quickly.

### Live responder coordination
Reports can be claimed and updated by community responders, making WOOF a lightweight coordination tool for local rescue networks rather than a standalone emergency service.

### First-aid and safety support
WOOF includes practical guidance for people handling animal distress situations, including when to seek veterinary help and how to act safely around an injured animal.

### Mobile-first design
The interface is built to be fast and readable for people responding in the field, especially on mobile devices while out in the community.

## WOOF in context

WOOF is not an ambulance, veterinary clinic, or licensed professional service. It is a coordination platform that helps community members and local responders find each other, share information, and move a case toward care.

The project is built around the idea that many animal emergencies are first noticed by everyday people who are not part of a formal rescue organization. WOOF gives them a practical way to act quickly, document what they see, and connect with nearby help.

## Project background

WOOF is a React + Vite app focused on helping local communities organize fast responses for stray and injured animals. It combines front-end rescue workflows with shared data updates, map-based context, and responder coordination.

The platform is intended to support humane, local, and practical rescue efforts—especially in communities where timely action can make a major difference for an animal's well-being.
